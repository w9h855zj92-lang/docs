PipeFitter runs entirely in your browser. There's no account to create, no app to install, and no subscription required. This guide walks you through opening the app and completing your first calculation — from a blank screen to a confirmed Travel and Cut Length in under two minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open PipeFitter">
    Navigate to [pipefitter.app](https://pipefitter.app) in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge all work. PipeFitter is fully responsive, so it works equally well on a desktop browser in the office or on a phone or tablet on the job site. After the first load, the app is cached and works without an active internet connection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a calculator">
    Tap or click the calculator you need from the home screen. Each calculator is listed with a short description so you can find the right tool at a glance. For your first calculation, try **Simple Offset** — it covers the most common single-plane offset scenario and is a good way to get familiar with how the app works.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your values">
    Type in the **Rise** — the perpendicular distance your pipe needs to travel around the obstacle. Then choose your **fitting angle**: tap one of the preset buttons (22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60°) or tap **Custom** to enter any angle. Results update live as you type, so you can adjust your inputs and watch the numbers change in real time without pressing a calculate button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read your results">
    **Travel**, **Run**, and **Cut Length** appear instantly in the results area below your inputs. The live diagram on the right updates to match your inputs, giving you a visual check that the geometry makes sense before you mark your pipe. Tap any result field to copy the value to your clipboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Units

Toggle between imperial (feet-inch-fractions) and metric using the unit switcher at the top of any calculator. In imperial mode, enter feet, inches, and fractions in separate fields — the custom numpad provides fraction buttons from 1/8 to 7/8 so you can match a tape measure reading directly. In metric mode, enter all values in millimetres. Switching units at any point converts all current inputs and outputs automatically — you never need to re-enter your numbers.

<Note>
  Use the **Reference Tables** alongside the calculators when you need to look up pipe dimensions or verify a fitting allowance. Open the Reference section from the main menu at any time — it doesn't reset your calculator inputs.
</Note>
