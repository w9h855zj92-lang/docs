The Spool Cut calculator converts your field measurements into the cut lengths you need to fabricate a spool piece. PipeFitter supports three measurement methods — Centre-to-Centre (CTC), Cut-to-End (CTE), and End-to-End (ETE) — and accounts for fitting take-out automatically.

## Measurement methods

Choose the measurement method that matches how you took your field dimensions:

| Method | Description |
|---|---|
| **CTC** (Centre-to-Centre) | Distance measured between the centres of two fittings. This is the most common field measurement method and the most precise when fittings are already installed. |
| **CTE** (Cut-to-End) | Distance from a pipe cut end to a fitting centre. Use this method when one end of the spool is a plain pipe end and the other terminates at a fitting. |
| **ETE** (End-to-End) | Distance between two pipe cut ends. This is the simplest measurement and requires no fitting references — useful when the fittings will be added after the spool is cut. |

## Inputs

<ParamField body="Measurement Type" type="select" required>
  Select your field measurement method: **CTC**, **CTE**, or **ETE**. The take-out calculation adjusts automatically based on your selection.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Measured Dimension" type="number" required>
  The distance you measured in the field, in feet-inches-fractions (imperial) or millimetres (metric). Enter exactly what your tape measure reads.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Fitting Type" type="select" required>
  The type of fitting at each end of the spool: **90° elbow**, **45° elbow**, **tee**, **cap**, or **flange**. The fitting type determines the take-out value applied at each end.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Pipe Size (NPS)" type="select" required>
  The nominal pipe size of the spool. Used to look up standard take-out values from ASME fitting tables.
</ParamField>

## Outputs

<ResponseField name="Cut Length" type="string">
  The pipe length to cut. This is the dimension you mark on the pipe — the measured dimension minus the applicable take-out at each end.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Take-Out (each end)" type="string">
  The fitting take-out value applied at each end of the spool. Displayed for both ends so you can verify the values match your actual fittings before cutting.
</ResponseField>

## Example

The following example calculates the cut length for a spool measured centre-to-centre at 24", with 2-inch NPS 90° long-radius elbows on both ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the measurement type">
    Set **Measurement Type** to `CTC`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the measured dimension">
    Set **Measured Dimension** to `24"`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the fitting type">
    Set **Fitting Type** to `90° LR Elbow` on both ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the pipe size">
    Set **Pipe Size (NPS)** to `2"`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    For a 2-inch NPS 90° long-radius elbow, the ASME take-out is **3"**. PipeFitter subtracts the take-out at both ends:

    | Output | Value |
    |---|---|
    | Take-Out (each end) | 3" |
    | Cut Length | 18" (24" − 3" − 3") |

    Mark the pipe at **18"** and cut. The finished spool, once the elbows are welded or threaded on, will span the full 24" centre-to-centre.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Double-check fitting take-out values against the **Pipe Dimensions** reference table before cutting, especially when working with non-standard or imported fittings whose dimensions may differ from ASME standards. A take-out error on both ends can add up quickly on a long spool.
</Tip>
